Google Ad Manager is a comprehensive, hosted ad management platform introduced by Google that helps medium to large publishers sell, manage, and report on their digital ad inventory (such as display, video, and mobile app ads).

Combines Two Tools: It merges an ad server and an ad exchange into a single, unified platform.

Unified Auctions: It runs on a first-price auction model, meaning the highest bidder wins the ad space.

Direct Sales & Programmatic: Publishers can manage direct ad deals with advertisers as well as programmatic sales where ad spaces are auctioned off automatically in real time.
