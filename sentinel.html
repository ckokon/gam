Modern web applications demand high responsiveness, scalability, and optimal monetisation strategies. As content delivery systems evolve, architects are turning to single-page application patterns combined with streaming server-side rendering to serve seamless digital experiences.

Traditional page reload models created friction for users while browsing multi-chapter or long-form investigative media. Continuous scrolling addresses this friction by dynamically appending new content streams into the DOM as the viewport approaches lower boundaries.

However, combining dynamic content delivery with digital advertising introduces technical challenges regarding ad refresh limits, viewability metrics, and programmatic compliance enforced by platforms such as Google Ad Manager (GAM).

Article 2: The Future of Dynamic Monetization

AL

Alex Lemons

Published Oct 24, 2026

Monetising continuous scroll experiences requires strict adherence to buyer expectations and platform regulations. When ads refresh without clear user engagement or explicit content transitions, viewability scores degrade, leading to lower eCPMs and potential account suspensions.

By utilizing native browser APIs such as the IntersectionObserver, developers can detect exactly when a reader transitions into a secondary article block. This ensures that the dynamic refresh call to Google Publisher Tag (GPT) is tied directly to a legitimate user action.

Furthermore, implementing minimum interval debounces (such as preventing refreshes within a 30-second window or validating 50%+ viewport exposure) protects publisher health while maximizing programmatic yield across multi-article page sessions.
